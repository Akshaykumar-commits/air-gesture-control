GET STARTED

Ready in a minute.

 

1. Download GestureControlSetup.exe from the latest release. 2. Run it and open Air Gesture Control from the Start Menu. 3. Press Start and raise your hand in good light.
Windows warning? The app is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.
Safety. Move the real mouse to the top-left corner to stop instantly, or make a fist to lock. Needs Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and a webcam. Your camera video never leaves your PC.
