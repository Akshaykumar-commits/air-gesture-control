Privacy Policy

Air Gesture Control, last updated 1 October 2026.

What the app does

Air Gesture Control uses your webcam to detect your hand and turn gestures into mouse and keyboard actions on your own PC.

Camera

Camera video is processed in real time on your device only. It is not saved, recorded, uploaded or shared with anyone.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it works without an internet connection.

Settings

Your preferences (camera choice, cursor speed, chosen apps) are saved in a small file on your own PC and are never sent anywhere.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Akshaykumar-commits/air-gesture-control.
